Security

Your code. Your device. Your keys.

IDEom is built so that you don't have to trust anyone — us included. Projects stay local, backups are encrypted before they leave the device, and what the AI changes is up to you.

Your data

What's yours stays with you.

Zero-knowledge backup

Cloud backups are encrypted on your device with AES-256 before they're uploaded. Only you know the passphrase — we cannot open your backups. Genuinely cannot: it's technically impossible, not just promised.

The flip side, honestly: lose the passphrase and nobody gets into that backup again — us included. That's the price of real zero-knowledge, not an oversight.

Keys stay local

Your AI key and your provider tokens are stored on your device only. Provider sign-ins happen in the browser, directly with the provider — IDEom never sees your password.

No ads. No tracking.

No advertising, no analytics services, no data collection — neither in the app nor on this website. IDEom is funded by fair plans, not by your data.

Your control

Nothing happens without your yes.

Diff before writing

The AI never writes into your project unasked. You see every change as a diff first and decide yourself — accept or reject. No black box, no "trust me".

Snapshot net

Before every accepted change, IDEom saves the project state. Whatever the AI or you get up to — one tap and you're back on a working state. Experimenting without fear.

Honest warnings

Some free providers train on your requests. IDEom doesn't bury that in the fine print — it tells you right at the selection, so you decide with the cards on the table.

Model-open — you choose who reads along

IDEom doesn't tie you to any AI provider. You connect the provider of your choice — by key or by sign-in — and can switch at any time. Your chat messages and the project files involved go directly to that provider, never through our servers.
