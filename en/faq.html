Getting started & cost
Do I need my own AI key?

You need your own access to an AI provider — but that sounds more complicated than it is. With several providers you simply sign in (e.g. OpenRouter or GitHub), no key copying at all. IDEom walks you through the setup on first launch.

Why your own access at all? Because that way you are in control: your plan, your provider, your data — and IDEom doesn't have to cap or subsidise your usage.

Can I use IDEom completely free?

Yes. IDEom's free tier does everything essential — and there are AI providers with generous free quotas that IDEom suggests to you directly. That's enough to build real apps without spending a cent.

Honest note: some free providers train on your requests. IDEom shows you that openly at the selection, so you decide with the cards on the table.

Does IDEom work offline — with Ollama, for example?

The editor, building and installing work entirely offline — the build chain runs on your device after all. Only the AI agent needs a connection to a model.

You can use a local model for that too: if you run Ollama (say on a PC in your home network), you enter its address as a custom endpoint — then not a single request leaves your network.

Which Android version do I need?

IDEom is built for current Android devices and tested on real everyday hardware — not a super-device. We'll pin down the exact minimum version for the open test and state it here and on the download page then.

Your data & control
Does my code stay private?

Yes. Your projects live on your device, not on our servers. When you work with the agent, the files involved go directly to the AI provider you chose — never to us. Optional cloud backups are end-to-end encrypted before they leave your device; we can't open them.

Can the AI break my project?

It can make mistakes — any AI can. But broken in the sense of "unrecoverable" isn't possible in IDEom: first, you see every change as a diff and have to approve it. Second, the snapshot net saves the project state before every change — one tap and you're back on a working state.

Apps & what's ahead
What makes IDEom different from ChatGPT & co.?

A chatbot gives you code as text — and then what? On a phone you have nothing to build or run it with. IDEom closes exactly that gap: it's a real development environment with an editor, project management and a build chain. The agent doesn't just write code, it works inside your project, builds the app, reads compiler errors and fixes them — until something installable is standing there.

Can I publish my apps?

The apps IDEom builds are real, signed APKs — they belong to you. You can share and install them wherever you like. Publishing on the Play Store means Google's usual requirements (developer account, policies) — that's outside IDEom, but fundamentally possible with the apps it builds. A dedicated way to pass IDEom apps on to others is planned for later.

When is IDEom coming to the Play Store?

After the open alpha. First IDEom should prove itself in everyday use and collect feedback — then comes the store launch with the subscription tiers. You can see the honest state any time on the roadmap and in the workshop notes.

How do I report bugs, requests or questions?

Bugs are fastest via the bug report page — one click opens a prepared report on GitHub, no further sign-up.

Requests, ideas and questions belong in the GitHub discussions — for that you sign in once with your GitHub account. A proper community with an IDEom profile comes later. What becomes of it you'll see in the workshop notes.
