Roadmap

Where the journey goes.

IDEom starts with an app on a phone — but the idea is bigger. This page lays out where the journey can go: from the foundation that already runs today to the larger goals. Some of it is near and certain, some of it is still vision.

The route can change. The further down a stage sits, the more it's a destination and the less it's a firm commitment — order and details shift while we build and listen. What's actually happening right now is always in the workshop notes.
Today
The foundation stands. Building real apps on the phone: editor, AI agent with the diff flow (you approve every change), building & installing directly on the device, the snapshot net as a way back, and encrypted cloud backup. Already running in the alpha.
Next
More choice, more say, a voice. A curated selection of AI models that the community helps decide on. IDEom available to install in more places. Simpler access without having to set anything up yourself. James gets a voice — talk to him and listen at the press of a button. And the English edition.
The PC leap
IDEom on the computer too. IDEom comes to the PC — and working together, the computer takes on the heavy lifting while your phone stays the control. For bigger projects and more power.
The ecosystem
A marketplace — and James as a real companion. A marketplace where developers can offer their own apps and AI services. And James grows from a helper into a full companion you can actually talk to.
Off the grid
Independent of the internet. Small AI models running directly on the phone, so the core functions work without a stable connection — for everyone who doesn't have signal everywhere.
Long term
IDEom as a whole ecosystem. Not only programming: IDEom as an umbrella — for example IDEom Talk, free conversation with the model of your choice. Plus your profile with account, backups and community. One coherent whole.
