Everything is free during the alpha. Using the AI itself runs through your own provider access — and there are completely free routes there too. Prices for Desktop and Studio aren't fixed yet.

Why this way?

No bait model. An honest calculation.

Plenty of apps make the free version deliberately unusable so that you pay. Not IDEom: the compiler runs on your phone, the AI runs through your own access — your usage costs us almost nothing. The one place where real money appears is the cloud storage for your backups. That's exactly what the supporter model carries.

So there's no feature prison: you don't pay for permission to use IDEom, you pay because you want it to stay ad-free and independent — without us ever having to sell your data.

— honestly calculated, the IDEom workshop
