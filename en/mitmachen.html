Something broken? Say so.

Something crashing, failing to build or behaving strangely? Report it directly — every report gets read, and what comes of it shows up publicly in the workshop notes. No account with us needed.

🐛 Report a bug

The button opens a prepared report on GitHub — you only fill in the gaps: what happened, what you expected, and how to reproduce it.

Report a bug

Reporting currently needs a (free) GitHub account — a form without any account, and reporting straight from inside the app, are planned.

Why does it only say "report a bug" here? Because a broken state should always be reportable immediately and without a hurdle — before anything else, before any sign-up.

Requests, ideas and having a say on what's next arrive once IDEom has accounts: then you'll be able to comment and join in directly at the news and on the roadmap — with your own profile.
